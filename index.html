    🎯 Our Mission

    Herbora was created to bridge the gap between ancient herbal wisdom and modern life. We believe everyone deserves access to clear, reliable information about plants and their traditional healing properties — all in one beautifully simple app.

    We are not here to replace medicine. We are here to educate, inspire, and empower you to make informed choices about your natural health journey.
